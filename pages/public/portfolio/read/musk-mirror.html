The Musk Mirror

How one billionaire exposed the cracks in China's innovation dream

A gallery of expert predictions, set against what was actually built. The book reads as a mirror rather than a weapon: on the rocket sceptics, the communications 'experts', the business-model critics and the CEO chorus, and on what their confidence cost. Its argument is about expertise without humility, and about the difference between a claim that sounds authoritative and a number that can be checked.
