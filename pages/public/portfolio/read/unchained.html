Unchained Blueprint

What to build when every payment door is closed

Every single door was closed: the processor that does not support your country, the payouts that are unavailable in your region, the account that has been limited. This blueprint starts from the observation that these are decisions rather than accidents, and works out what a builder in that position can still put in place.
