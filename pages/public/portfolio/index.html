Order Without Custody

Governance-first design for merchant commerce rails in low-infrastructure economies

Small merchants already sell at scale — through chat threads, pages and a phone number painted on a wall — but they sell without a record. This paper argues that the binding constraint at that tier is not payment capability but reconciliation, and that reconcilability is an architectural property rather than a feature. Five mechanisms are formalised against artefacts a reader can inspect: the No-Custody Rail, Admission-Gated Discovery, Reserve-Ladder Scheduling, Surface-Disjoint Multi-Tenancy and the Constrained Persuasion Doctrine. The empirical section is forensic: five classes of governance failure, each stated as a signature, a detector and a design response.

Candid about its limits: no paying customers in the measurement window, no control condition, no inference beyond the case.

The Sovereign Micro-Enterprise

A governance-first architecture for zero-capital agentic businesses

Frontier models pushed the price of machine cognition toward zero, and the obvious inference — that any connected individual now owns a software company — proves fragile in practice. Producing is easy; operating is hard. This report documents the design, deployment and near-real-time failure forensics of a one-person, zero-capital sales and content business run entirely by autonomous agents under a governance architecture: guarded capability, a ledgered agent pattern with an idle witness, a zero-capital ladder, and effectuation-in-the-loop. Seven figures carry the anatomy, the cadence and the incident traces.

Silent-failure taxonomy with detection-time bounds, offered as reusable diagnostic vocabulary.

Grounded Cadence

Fact-bound content operations as a governance architecture

Anyone can now emit a week of social copy. Almost no one can emit it on a schedule, from a company's own facts, without inventing customers or numbers, without spending the operator's quota on a stranger, and without a silent failure looking like a green run. This paper reports a live zero-capital writing service and formalises four mechanisms with production incidents attached: fact-bound generation, value–promo cadence, capability isolation, and fail-loud operations.

Honest where it matters: the Worker is in production, the test surface is documented, paying customers are zero.

STPi

Architect & engineer · applied agent system

A hiring agent that hunts country-specific sources twice a day, Monday to Saturday, across South Africa, Zimbabwe, Zambia and fourteen other southern African countries. Every advert is scored against the candidate's real profile; a tailored cover letter and a country-formatted CV are drafted as PDFs; one card is posted to the dashboard. Approve it and it sends from the candidate's own mailbox, signed as the candidate, with the reply reaching both — interview invites flagged. Reject it and the PDFs are deleted on the spot. Nothing sends without a human line, and nothing is invented.

About

Jacaranda Labs Press · Harare

The writing starts where the assumptions imported into a market simply do not hold — always-on power, card rails, a postcode, a credit history. The building follows: autonomous cognitive infrastructure that assumes none of them, and keeps a record when the connection drops.

Autonomous Cognitive Infrastructure Engineer, Operator, Writer. Three descriptions of one job: design systems that act on their own, write down how they fail, and stay answerable for the run. The writing is governance-first architecture, evidence and constraint. The building is merchant rails, operator consoles, learning platforms, hiring infrastructure and publishing pipelines — each deployed, each documented in public.

No single product defines the practice. Tapstall is one of the systems that came out of it, and the books are another: the catalogue above is the written half, and the systems above that are the running half.

The books are published under a Creative Commons licence where I hold the rights, and produced as Markdown first. The page you are reading was generated by a script from the same repository the books live in, so the catalogue cannot drift from the files.
