Privacy · no cookies, no analytics

Privacy

Nothing here profiles you. This page says exactly what happens to a visit, what happens to a message, and where the site's responsibility stops and the host's begins.

The short version

This site is a folder of HTML files. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, loads no scripts, fonts or images from anyone else's server, and has no advertising on it. Nothing about you is collected by the site itself. That is not a promise bolted on at the end — it is the reason the site can be checked: view the source of any page and there is nothing to find.

What the host sees

The pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it receives the request: your IP address, the page asked for, the time, your browser's user-agent, and any referrer. GitHub holds that in its own logs under GitHub's privacy statement, not under this site's control. Downloads are served from GitHub's release hosting for the same reason — the file transfer is GitHub's, and it sees the request that fetches it.

No IP address, referrer or user-agent from those logs is copied into this repository, and nothing here can read them.

If you write

Email goes to couragegarikai@atomicmail.io. Calls, WhatsApp and Telegram go to the two numbers on the contact page — those conversations are held by your phone network and by those applications, under their terms as well as mine.

What is kept: the message, so it can be answered, and the thread afterwards, so a second conversation does not start from nothing. What is not done: no address is added to a mailing list, there is no newsletter to unsubscribe from, and no message is sold, rented or handed to a marketing tool.

The contact button

The floating button at the bottom of the page is an animated image. It links to WhatsApp; nothing is sent, and WhatsApp is not opened, until you click it. The small circle beside it starts a phone call in the same way.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics, tag manager, session recording, heat map or A/B test.
  • No cookies — first-party or third-party. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.
  • No advertising, sponsorships, affiliate links or retargeting pixels.
  • No fingerprinting, and no attempt to identify a reader from their device.
  • No embedded video, map, font or widget that would phone home on load.
  • No email capture, pop-up, exit-intent modal or chat widget.

The products have their own pages

Tapstall, Open Practice, ContentOps, DeskOS, STPi and the Bluesky Growth Audit are separate systems with their own statements about what they collect. This page covers this site only, and it does not speak for them.

Your rights, in plain terms

Because this site holds nothing, there is nothing here to export or erase. If you have written to me, you can ask what is held and ask for it to be deleted; a reply saying what was done is enough, and no form is needed. If you are in a jurisdiction that gives you a formal right of access or erasure, write and cite it — it will be honoured the same way.

Children

The site is written for adults working on systems. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes
