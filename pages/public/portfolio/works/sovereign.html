The Sovereign Micro-Enterprise

A governance-first architecture for zero-capital agentic businesses

Working manuscript

Frontier models pushed the price of machine cognition toward zero, and the obvious inference — that any connected individual now owns a software company — proves fragile in practice. Producing is easy; operating is hard. This report documents the design, deployment and near-real-time failure forensics of a one-person, zero-capital sales and content business run entirely by autonomous agents under a governance architecture: guarded capability, a ledgered agent pattern with an idle witness, a zero-capital ladder, and effectuation-in-the-loop. Seven figures carry the anatomy, the cadence and the incident traces.

Silent-failure taxonomy with detection-time bounds, offered as reusable diagnostic vocabulary.
