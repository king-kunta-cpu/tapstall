    Order Without Custody

    Governance-first design for merchant commerce rails in low-infrastructure economies. Small merchants already sell at scale — through chat threads, pages and a phone number painted on a wall — but they sell without a record. This paper argues that the binding constraint at that tier is not payment capability but reconciliation, and that reconcilability is an architectural property rather than a feature. Five mechanisms are formalised against artefacts a reader can inspect: the No-Custody Rail, Admission-Gated Discovery, Reserve-Ladder Scheduling, Surface-Disjoint Multi-Tenancy and the Constrained Persuasion Doctrine. The empirical section is forensic: five classes of governance failure, each stated as a signature, a detector and a design response.

    The Sovereign Micro-Enterprise

    A governance-first architecture for zero-capital agentic businesses. Frontier models pushed the price of machine cognition toward zero, and the obvious inference — that any connected individual now owns a software company — proves fragile in practice. Producing is easy; operating is hard. This report documents the design, deployment and near-real-time failure forensics of a one-person, zero-capital sales and content business run entirely by autonomous agents under a governance architecture: guarded capability, a ledgered agent pattern with an idle witness, a zero-capital ladder, and effectuation-in-the-loop. Seven figures carry the anatomy, the cadence and the incident traces.

    Grounded Cadence

    Fact-bound content operations as a governance architecture. Anyone can now emit a week of social copy.
