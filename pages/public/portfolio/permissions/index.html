Permissions, press and citation

What may be quoted, what may be reused, and how to credit it — stated per work rather than in a paragraph of lawyer's fog.

The site itself

The writing on these pages is © Courage Garikai, 2026, unless a work says otherwise. Short quotation with attribution and a link is welcome and needs no permission. Republishing a page whole, or feeding this site into a product that resells it, is not covered by that.

The design, the mark, the jacket artwork, the share cards and the illustrations are made for these works. Use them to write about the works — a review, a citation, a course page — and do not use them as anyone else's branding.

How to cite

Papers and books, in a form that survives being copied:

Courage Garikai (2026). Order Without Custody: Governance-First Design for Merchant Commerce Rails in Low-Infrastructure Economies. DCAI working paper 3. Jacaranda Labs Think Tank. https://king-kunta-cpu.github.io/tapstall/portfolio/works/custody.html

For a web page: author, title of the page, the site, the date you read it, the URL. Every work page is stable — it is regenerated from the same catalogue entry, not renumbered — so a link from a syllabus will not rot into a 404.

Teaching, and course use

Works marked CC BY 4.0 may be used in teaching with attribution — no email needed, though knowing where they are being taught is useful. For works marked all rights reserved, ask; permission for a class is normally given the same day.

Translations, editions, and other uses
