What a payment method costs. The built-in table is the handful of tariffs we verified for Zimbabwe and South Africa; anything typed here is live for new orders in seconds and nothing is redeployed. That is the whole point — a merchant on a method we have never heard of should still be quoted the right number, and we should not have to ship code to serve a new country.
