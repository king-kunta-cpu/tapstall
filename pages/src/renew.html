Nothing of yours is a hostage

  • Your menu lives in your own Google Sheet and your own account. We read it; we never hold it.

  • We built this here only because the mainstream providers would not serve shops like ours — declined cards, “country not supported”, priced for California. Keeping your data is the same promise as that one.
