# Your first week on TapStall — the operator guide

Welcome. TapStall is your online shop: one link, your menu, orders that tell you exactly who paid.
Your money always goes straight to your own EcoCash / PayShap / bank / wallet — TapStall never holds
it. This page is the whole manual. Twenty minutes today, then just share your link.

## Day 1 — make the shop real (20 minutes)

1. **Open your admin link** (the private link you got when you signed up — save it as a bookmark,
   it is your key; anyone who has it can edit your shop).
2. **Set your payment details** if you have not: the account your customers must pay, exactly as it
   should appear. This prints on every order, so double-check the digits.
3. **Add at least five items** with real prices. Your best sellers first. A shop with five priced
   items takes orders; a shop with two items and no prices does not.
4. If you already keep your menu in a Google Sheet, you can connect it instead — the admin page
   shows how. Otherwise the in-app menu is enough.
5. **Open your shop link yourself** (the public one) and look at it like a customer. Fix anything
   that looks wrong now, not after you share it.

## Day 1, continued — place a test order on yourself

Before any customer sees it:

1. Open your shop link, add an item, place an order with your own name.
2. Notice the amount: it has **odd cents** (like $23.47, not $23.50). That is on purpose — every
   order gets a unique amount, so when money lands in your account you know which order paid.
   No more arguing about screenshots.
3. Go to your admin page, open the order, and tap **Confirm payment** (pretend you received it).
   Watch it move to paid. Then cancel the test if you like — the point was to feel the loop once.

## Days 2–3 — put the link where people already look

- **WhatsApp status** with today's special and the link. Do this daily; it is your shop window.
- Your **Facebook page/profile** and any groups you sell in.
- **Print the QR code** (your admin page offers print sheets) and put it at your stand, counter,
  or on packaging. One by the till, one facing the queue.
- Send it to your **last ten happy customers** with one line: "You can order straight from my link
  now — quicker for both of us."

When a customer orders, they pay **your account directly**, then TapStall hands them to your
WhatsApp to finish (name, collection/delivery details). When the money lands, match the odd-cents
amount to the order and tap Confirm. That tap also keeps your books.

## Days 4–7 — small habits that compound

- **Update prices and sold-outs the moment they change.** A correct menu prevents angry messages.
- **Confirm payments the same day.** The customer sees the confirmed state, and your books
  (admin → books) stay true: what sold, what is owed, what came in.
- Check the **receipt/proof link** on a paid order — you can print it or send it to the customer.
- If someone pays the wrong amount, talk to them on WhatsApp as you always did; confirm only when
  the money is right.

## Your 60 free days, and paying after

Your shop runs free for 60 days. After that it is **$60 for a year** — flat, no commission on your
sales, ever. Your admin page shows your days left and your **renew link**.

To renew: open the renew link and follow it. If you pay by bank transfer/EcoCash outside the page,
use **"file a payment reference"** on the renew page and paste the reference from your payment —
a person checks it and your year is added, usually the same day. Keep your own proof until you see
the new expiry date in admin.

If your days run out, your shop pauses (customers see a notice). Your menu and orders are not
deleted; renewing wakes everything up.

## Keeping your keys safe

- Your **admin link** and **shop PIN** (if you use one) are the keys to your shop. Do not share
  them in groups or screenshots. Staff who need to confirm payments can use the confirm link on
  each order instead of your admin link.
- Lost your links? Ask the person who signed you up — the owner can re-issue them, and old links
  can be killed if they leaked.

## When to ask for help

- Money landed but no order matches the amount → check the cents carefully; still stuck, message
  your seller with the amount and time.
- Your menu looks old → if you use a Google Sheet feed, make sure the sheet is shared "anyone with
  the link can view" and wait a few minutes; in-app edits show immediately.
- Anything about your subscription or expiry → your seller, or the owner through your renew page.
