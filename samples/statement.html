The two facts this page is built on

Expected is what the order book says buyers owed. Matched is what the shop recorded as actually received against an order. They are kept apart on purpose: a rail that shows a payment is not the same thing as money in the shop’s account, and this page never calls one the other.

Every rail on this statement paid the shop directly. Tapstall does not hold the money, does not pay it out and does not convert it — it reads what the shop recorded and does the arithmetic. Rows stay available for five years. A rail that is missing from this page has no payments recorded against it, which is a fact worth checking rather than a gap in the arithmetic.
